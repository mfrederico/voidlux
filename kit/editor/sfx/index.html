Sound effects

Pick a sound, shape it, hear it. Every sound is a stack of layers: a wave sweeping from one pitch to another, fading over its length, starting after its delay. Opening a sound reads how the game plays it now; your edits replace it in the game once you save (reload the game to hear them). Sounds you never touch stay exactly as the game codes them.
